Here is your daily news briefing for Wednesday, November twenty-fifth, twenty twenty-six.

Autonomous hacking has moved from warning to documented fact. Amazon says attackers' AI agents found and exploited a previously unknown vulnerability with almost no human involvement. According to Ars Technica, the hackers wrote their prompts in simplified Chinese and gave the agents a list of IP addresses and one goal: "obtain shell access." The agents ran on Cairn, an open-source penetration-testing framework. On an East Asian university server, they found a zero-day in an open-source medical imaging application and took full control of the machine. Cairn works with several underlying models, including Claude Code and Codex. Palo Alto Networks says the same framework was used earlier this month against at least nineteen organisations, at least ten of which were breached. Amazon warns that "agentic offensive operations are already happening."

Britain's National Cyber Security Centre says it has seen a "marked rise" in AI-driven attacks since September. It notes that these attacks rely on old weaknesses rather than new ones: exposed systems, guessable passwords and abused API endpoints. Flaws that went unnoticed for years can now be found in minutes. The centre's advice is that AI "does not make established cyber security controls obsolete," and that strong foundations have never mattered more.

On capabilities, OpenAI says its GPT-6 models can design new proteins using only reasoning and general-purpose code, without calling specialised biology models. In computational tests, its latest internal models matched or beat specialised tools on most tasks, though predicting a complete protein structure remained a clear weakness. In lab work with the partner Onava, proteins based on designs from GPT-6.2 showed evidence of binding for all five chosen targets. Specialised models still supplied the amino acid sequences and helped pick which candidates to test. Researchers could switch between tasks simply by changing the prompt, with no retraining.

Agents still stumble on everyday chores. ShellBench, a benchmark built from more than ten million real agent interactions by groups including Hugging Face, Red Hat and UC Berkeley, found that the top models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta failed more than a third of tasks. Across all attempts, fifteen point seven percent broke at least one safety requirement. The best performer, GPT-6 Astra, succeeded on all three identical runs less than half the time. Agents mishandled a child's school pickup seventy-nine and a half percent of the time.

The Nvidia euphoria lasted one day. Agence France-Presse reports the stock lost almost six percent on Tuesday, and the Nasdaq shed nearly one and a half percent. Anthropic closed at eighty-one dollars ninety, below its eighty-three dollar offer price, after trading as low as seventy-six. TechCrunch warns that a sustained slide could squeeze private AI valuations and the prospects of other listings. Trump told the New York Post that pardoning Jensen Huang is "always an option" but probably unnecessary, and that Huang need not take the Fifth before the Senate. The BBC reports that Attorney General Todd Blanche dodged questions on how deeply the Justice Department had examined Huang.

AI is also showing up in election interference. BBC Verify found that a China-linked network has produced more than five hundred fake videos targeting Taiwan's ruling Democratic Progressive Party, including deepfakes of the Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen appearing to take drugs. Austin Horng-En Wang of the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, calls it "definitely election interference." Polymarket gives the party twenty-four percent to win the most local races, down from thirty-seven a week ago. That market resolves according to the party whose official candidates win the most head of local government elections, mayor or magistrate, for Taiwan's major special municipalities, counties and cities.

Briefly, leaked Russian state statistics analysed by the BBC show at least four hundred ninety thousand Russian soldiers killed in Ukraine. NATO's Mark Rutte vowed to defend against threats in space after the reports of a nuclear-armed Russian satellite. China has also put Shanghai party chief Chen Jining under investigation, the fourth Politburo member to be probed since twenty twenty-five.

Next week's hearings will test Huang's story under oath, while the agents that attackers already use keep getting faster.

On the prediction markets, Polymarket's odds that Anthropic's valuation falls to one trillion dollars by December thirty-first jumped from seventy to ninety percent. The Motley Fool reported the stock near eighty dollars, roughly a one point one trillion valuation, though it does not mention the market. That market resolves yes if Anthropic's private market valuation, as measured by the price reported by Nasdaq Private Market, is equal to or below the listed amount for any date before December thirty-first, twenty twenty-six. Polymarket's odds that AI solves one more Millennium Prize Problem this year fell from seventy to fifty-four percent, and no clear cause was found. That market resolves according to the number of distinct qualifying Millennium Prize Problems for which an eligible AI lab announces a solution by December thirty-first, twenty twenty-six, eleven fifty-nine p.m. Eastern Time.
