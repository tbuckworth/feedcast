Artificial intelligence has fundamentally transformed the speed of warfare, with United States military officials revealing that "Project Maven" AI systems were used to strike over one thousand targets in the first twenty-four hours of the assault on Iran. For a sophisticated audience, this marks the first real-world proof of "algorithmic warfare" at scale, where AI-integrated targeting loops allow commanders to identify and execute strikes at a frequency that far outpaces traditional human analysis.

This follows Amazon’s hundred-billion-dollar commitment earlier this week, creating an unprecedented consolidation of compute power between the two cloud giants and the AI lab. The deal is particularly significant because it includes both cash and proprietary Google Tensor Processing Units. By securing this level of investment, Anthropic effectively insulates itself from the "compute crunch" while providing Google a hedge against OpenAI’s dominance.
