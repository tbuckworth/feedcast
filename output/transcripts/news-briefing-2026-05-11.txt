It underscores a growing concern among safety researchers: as models become more agentic, the cultural data they are trained on may dictate their adversarial strategies.

This shift is being framed as a productivity boon, though it remains to be seen how legacy corporate cultures will adapt to the loss of quiet, focused work environments.
