This is significant because these warnings are not coming from outside critics — they are coming from the people building the systems, and the political response is now bipartisan and urgent.

US government debt now exceeds forty trillion dollars. Meanwhile, oil prices climbed back above one hundred dollars a barrel as the Iran conflict continued to escalate. Brent crude is up more than sixty percent this year. The UN nuclear watchdog referred Iran to the Security Council for non-compliance with the non-proliferation treaty for the first time in twenty years, citing lack of access to nuclear facilities and insufficient information about Iran's highly enriched uranium stockpile.
